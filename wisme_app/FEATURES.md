# 🚀 Wisme App - Complete Feature Set

*AI-Powered Microlearning Platform with Personalized Voice Coaches*

## 📱 **Features by User Experience Flow**

### **1. ONBOARDING & DISCOVERY**

#### **🎯 Smart First Launch Experience**
- **Welcome Animation**: Interactive AI coach introduction
- **Value Proposition Screen**: "Learn anything in bite-sized lessons"
- **Permission Setup**: Audio access, notifications (with clear explanations)

#### **🧠 AI-Powered Topic Discovery**
- **Intelligent Search**: Type any topic to learn about
- **Dynamic Topic Analysis**: AI assesses topic complexity in real-time
- **Trending Suggestions**: Popular learning topics updated daily
- **Category Browse**: Business, Tech, Health, Creative, Finance, Lifestyle

#### **⚡ Dynamic Knowledge Level Selection**
**For Professional/Complex Topics:**
- Concepts & Fundamentals
- Real Case Studies & Examples  
- Complete Deep Dive (Theory + Practice)

**For General/Broad Topics:**
- Smart Specificity Selector: "Want to learn something specific or general overview?"
- AI-Generated Subtopic Suggestions based on the topic
- Adaptive questioning to narrow focus

#### **🎭 Contextual Coach Selection**
- **Topic-Matched Coaches**: AI recommends coaches best suited for chosen subject
- **6 Unique AI Personalities**: Zen Coach, Startup Buddy, Science Guide, Motivational, Storyteller, Default
- **Voice Previews**: Hear each coach teaching YOUR specific topic
- **Personality Descriptions**: "Perfect for [your topic]" contextual explanations
- **Podcast-Style Narration**: Engaging, conversational delivery like your favorite podcast host

#### **📅 AI-Generated Learning Journey**
- **10-Day Learning Plan**: AI creates comprehensive topic mastery roadmap
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Each day builds on previous knowledge
- **Flexible Duration**: Extend to 15, 20, or 30 days based on depth needed
- **Journey Preview**: See the complete learning arc before starting
- **Daily Themes**: Each day focuses on specific aspect (fundamentals → case studies → advanced applications)

**Example 10-Day Journey: "Startup Funding Mastery"**
- Day 1: "Funding Fundamentals - What Every Founder Needs to Know"
- Day 2: "Bootstrap vs. VC - Choosing Your Path"
- Day 3: "Crafting Your Pitch Deck - The Essential Elements"
- Day 4: "Case Study: How Airbnb Survived Near-Death to Raise $112M"
- Day 5: "Valuation Basics - What's Your Startup Really Worth?"
- Day 6: "Case Study: The $19B WhatsApp Deal - Lessons for Every Founder"
- Day 7: "Due Diligence - What Investors Really Look For"
- Day 8: "Negotiating Terms - Getting the Best Deal"
- Day 9: "Post-Funding Strategy - Making the Most of Your Investment"
- Day 10: "Scaling Challenges - Preparing for Series B and Beyond"

#### **🚀 Journey Customization**
- **Extend Anytime**: "Want to go deeper? Add 5 more days"
- **Smart Continuation**: Near day 10, AI suggests advanced topics
- **Related Journeys**: "Ready for 'Advanced Marketing Strategies'?"
- **Flexible Pacing**: Complete at your own speed (1 lesson/day recommended)

#### **🎙️ Detailed Journey Experience Flow**

**Post Knowledge-Level Selection:**
1. **AI Journey Generation** (3-5 seconds loading)
   - "🧠 Creating your personalized learning journey..."
   - "📅 Designing a 10-day mastery plan..."
   - "🎙️ Crafting podcast-style episodes..."

2. **Journey Preview & Coach Selection**
   - Complete 10-day episode breakdown with titles
   - Coach selection with topic-specific previews
   - Extension options (15, 20, 30 days)
   - Focus customization (case studies vs theory balance)

3. **Journey Commitment & First Episode**
   - Daily reminder settings
   - First episode preview with podcast-style introduction
   - Download options for offline learning

**Podcast-Style Narration Examples:**

*Episode Opening (Startup Buddy):*
"Welcome back to your Startup Funding Mastery journey! I'm your host, and if you're just joining us, we're on Day 4... Yesterday, we covered pitch deck essentials – and wow, the response was incredible! Today, I want to tell you a story that'll blow your mind..."

*Mid-Episode Engagement:*
"Now, here's where it gets really interesting... [pause] The investors who'd rejected Airbnb just months before were now fighting to get into their Series A. What changed? I'll give you a hint: it had nothing to do with revenue..."

*Episode Ending with Cliffhanger:*
"Tomorrow, we're exploring WhatsApp's $19 billion acquisition – and you'll discover how they used this exact same principle, but in a completely different way. Trust me, you won't want to miss that episode..."

**Journey Extension Logic:**
- **Day 8-9 Check-in**: Smart extension prompts with advanced topics
- **Day 10 Completion**: Certificate, stats, and next journey recommendations
- **Flexible Extensions**: Add 3-5 days anytime during journey
- **Related Journey Suggestions**: Cross-topic learning paths
